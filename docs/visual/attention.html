How to read it

  • A token's row is what it gathers while working out the next character. So = is the step that writes the first answer digit.
  • Attention says where information is pulled from, not what is done with it. A strong look is a clue, not proof.
  • Things to look for: does = look at the innermost brackets on early passes and outer ones later? Do heads split jobs (one on the previous token, one on operators)? Does deep do the same over its layers?
  • Every answer here is correct. The models are the 120k-step runs, seed 0. Data comes from scripts/attn_export.py.
