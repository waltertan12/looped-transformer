Looped transformer · training curves

No model here has finished learning

Every run was still improving when it stopped, including the longest (120k steps). So our results compare models at the same training budget, not at their best.

What the curves say

  • Accuracy climbs almost in a straight line to the end. From step 80k to 120k, held-out accuracy rose +0.11 to +0.12 for deep, +0.08 to +0.13 for looped and +0.08 to +0.09 for shallow. A finished model would have flattened out.
  • Training loss is still falling too, even though the learning rate had been turned down to a tenth by the end.
  • Training and test accuracy stay close (0.52 vs 0.45 for looped), so it isn't memorizing. It just needs more steps. 120k steps of 256 problems is about 3 passes over the 10 million training problems.
  • What this changes. "Deep beats looped on numbers" could partly be "deep learns faster". A run to convergence on one model of each kind would tell the two apart.
